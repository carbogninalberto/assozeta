# Balance sheet and account delete — implementing with HTTP parity

`GET/POST/DELETE balance-sheet` (`balance_sheet`) and
`DELETE balance-sheet/accounts/<uid>/delete` (`balance_sheet_accounts_delete`)
in `BE/application/views/balance_sheet_views.py` are implemented in Go
(`BE_GO/internal/{domain,repository,service,handler}/balance_sheet*.go`).
Django remains the production execution owner.

## Behavior

- **Identity:** `IsAuthenticated` and `IsAssociationUser` (athletes get DRF's
  generic 403); the registry maps GET to `bookeeping.management.balancesheet.read`
  and POST/DELETE to `...balancesheet.update`, the account delete to
  `bookeeping.management.accounts.delete`. In `balance_sheet` a non-association
  role and a missing `user.sportassociation` (the reverse one-to-one accessor,
  base manager) are `403 {"msg": "Info not available."}`; the account delete
  uses `SportAssociation.objects.get` (active manager): missing is a 500.
- **GET** (and POST with a non-null `format`): `?currentDate` through
  `strptime('%Y-%m-%d')` (default today; invalid is a 500), the balance year from
  the user's start day/month, six `available_years` ending with today's year. The
  association's newest sheet of that year (archived included) is returned as
  stored when approved. Otherwise the stored (or default) data is regenerated by
  `generate_balance_sheet`: paid, amount > 0, live, non-archived payments through
  the group-aware manager whose `payment_date` (or `creation_date` when null) is
  in `[start, end 23:59:59]`; their categories are reset to 0, then `float(amount)`
  is added, missing categories appended and `meta_payment_categories` amounts
  moved (categories resolved by id; unknown ones skipped). Any exception inside
  the generation is swallowed and the partial data kept (Go ports the algorithm
  over a mutable Python value model: dict/list/str semantics, int vs float,
  `KeyError`/`TypeError`/UUID `ValidationError` aborts). Then `bank`/`cash`/`other`
  are float sums of the per-account Decimal balances up to the end date at 00:00
  and `total`; non-dict data is a 500. The sheet is created (audited create) or
  saved (audited update when a field changed).
- **POST** without `format`: exactly one key `balance_sheet` (else 400
  `Invalid data.`); `draft` read before the lookup, the newest sheet of any year
  (none: 404 `No balance sheet found.`), then `year`. Data replaced, status from
  draft truthiness. auditlog writes in `pre_save`: a year `int()` rejects (or an
  UPDATE PostgreSQL rejects) leaves the update entry and answers 500. The
  response echoes the raw year and draft.
- **DELETE**: resets the year's sheet to the default data (no sheet: 200
  `No balance sheet found.`).
- **Account delete**: the association's account (malformed/foreign/unknown:
  404 `{"exception": ...}`), non-editable is 403 `This account is not deletable.`;
  then `custom_account.delete()`, Django's deletion collector (Go runs the shared
  collector, `collaborator_delete_collector.go`): the account, the transfers
  referencing it and every payment of the account (base manager: payments removed
  from the list, of a group or never paid too) are deleted, each with a delete
  entry indexed through `changes_json`, in that order, the payments in descending
  key order. Before anything is deleted the payments' delete receiver runs: the
  instalments of a payment are deleted, and an instalment's own receiver deletes
  its unpaid payment first. That payment is audited, then the instalment (indexed
  to the actor's association, none for a collaborator), then the account's own
  pass audits the payment again.

## Excel export (POST with `format` `excel`)

On a non-approved sheet the view regenerates and saves the sheet as for GET,
then answers `export_balance_sheet`: 200 with `{'file': <base64 workbook>,
'filename': '[<dd/mm/YYYY today>] Bilancio <year> - <denomination>.xlsx'}`. An
approved sheet is returned as stored, whatever the format. The workbook is
`get_excel_base64` (`xlsxexport.PandasOpenpyxl`) of Descrizione, Istituzionale
(€), Commerciale (€), Totale (€): the income entries and their totals, the
expense entries and their totals, the management statement (income, minus
expenses, balance) and the liquidity rows (bank, cash, other, total), amounts
as `f"{x:.2f}"` with a comma. Rows follow the stored lists, so their order is
the data's own. An exception in the export (a missing key, an amount `float()`
rejects, a balance year starting on 29 February) is a 500 after the save: the
saved sheet and its audit entry stay. Go builds the workbook from the same
in-memory data before saving, so a refusal happens before any write.

## PDF export (POST with `format` `pdf`)

The same flow with `generate_pdf_from_data` (`pdfexport.DataTable`, see
[pdf-exports](pdf-exports.md)): 200 with `{'file': <base64 document>,
'filename': '[<dd/mm/YYYY today>] Bilancio <year> - <denomination>.pdf'}`. The
document is A3 portrait (larger when the table needs it), titled `Bilancio
<year> al <dd/mm/YYYY>` (the day before the next balance year starts), and
holds:

- a header table read back from the HTML header the view composes: the logo
  when the association's `logo` is a `data:image/jpeg;base64,` URL (drawn 100
  points high), otherwise the paragraph `[LOGO]`; the denomination (Helvetica
  Bold 20), `<city>, <postal code>, <address>` (16) and `Approvato il
  ____/____/______` (16), each wrapped as reportlab wraps a paragraph. A
  denomination with a line break does not match the view's pattern and is
  replaced by `Associazione Sportiva`, as in Django;
- the four columns (description left, amounts right) with the workbook's rows;
- the centred line `I saldi sono riportati al <dd/mm/YYYY>.`.

An exception of the export is the same 500 after the save as for `excel`.

## Not ported (refused before any write)

- In the exports: a non-finite amount, a description that is not text or
  null, and the workbook inputs `xlsxexport` does not reproduce
  (`xlsx-exports.md`).
- In the `pdf` export: a denomination, city, postal code or address containing
  `&`, `<` or a soft hyphen (reportlab parses paragraph text as markup; only
  plain text is ported), and a `data:image/jpeg;base64,` logo that is not a
  grey or colour 8-bit JPEG in strict base64 (reportlab would decode other
  images to pixels, or fall back to `[LOGO]` when PIL cannot open the data).
  Go answers 500 before generating.
- `CustomAccountSerializer.save` / `CustomAccountTransferSerializer.save` are not
  reached by these routes; they are exercised by the balance-account-writes and
  balance-transfer-writes families (account/transfer add and update).

## Evidence (TDD)

`TESTS/fixtures/balance_sheet_http.json` (generator alongside) authors 66 cases.
RED: Django 66/66, Go 404 (`native-r3`). GREEN: native Django, native Go, Caddy
`/api/v1` and `/api/v2` pass 66/66 with exact effects (`versioned-r1`).
Mutation: including archived payments in the generation fails
`draft-regenerated` (`mutation-r1`). Re-run green: balance-accounts,
balance-archived, balance-account-writes, balance-transfer-writes, testimonials.

Excel export: 11 more cases (77 in all). RED `v2xlsx-red-r2`: Django 77/77, Go
500 on `excel-draft`. GREEN `v2xlsx-green-final`: 77/77 on native Django, native
Go, Caddy `/api/v1` and `/api/v2` with exact effects. Mutation
`v2xlsx-mutation-final`: the expenses of the management statement without their
minus sign fail `excel-draft` (sheet1.xml differs).

Account delete with payments: 3 more cases (80 in all; accounts with payments
removed from the list, of a group and unpaid, with a transfer, and with an unpaid
payment and its course instalment). RED `v2del-red-r1`: Django 80/80, Go 500 on
`account-deleted-with-payments`. GREEN `v2del-versioned-r1` (and `v2del-final-r1`
after merging `codex/go-backend-parity`): 80/80 on native
Django, native Go, Caddy `/api/v1` and `/api/v2`. Mutation `v2del-mutation-r1`:
the payments' delete receiver skipped leaves the instalment in place
(`state.deleted:application.CourseSubscriptionInstallment`). Re-run green
(`v2del-rerun-r1`): balance-account-writes 29/29, balance-transfer-writes 30/30,
balance-accounts 25/25.

PDF export: 9 more cases (86 in all; the seeded association has no logo and no
address). RED `v2pdf-red-r1`: Django 86/86, Go 500 on `pdf-draft`. GREEN
`v2pdf-versioned-r1`: 86/86 on native Django, native Go, Caddy `/api/v1` and
`/api/v2` with exact effects, each document compared in decoded form. Mutation
`v2pdf-mutation-r1`: balances dated the first day of the next balance year
instead of the day before fail `pdf-draft`. The logo, long and unbroken
denominations, several pages and the closing line on a page of its own are
covered by the golden documents of `BE_GO/internal/pdfexport`.
